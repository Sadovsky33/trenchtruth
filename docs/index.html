FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Does it trade for me or hold my funds?

No. TrenchTruth only reads public on-chain data. It never asks for a private key, a seed phrase or a deposit.

How do you know a funder is a rug network?

We take the wallet that sent the creator its first SOL and look at every other creator it funded in our database of 15,008 launches. If those creators launched at least two coins and at least 80% died within five minutes, the funder is flagged. A coin's outcome enters the database only after it happened.

Is this financial advice?

No. It shows on-chain history and what happened to buyers before. Most memecoins lose money for the people who buy them, and a clean card is not a promise.

Which coins does it cover?

pump.fun coins on Solana today. Other launchpads are next.

Can I add it to my group?

Yes. Add the bot to a Telegram group and anyone can paste an address to get a card for the whole group.

Is it open source?

Yes. The bot, the scanner and our Solana shred decoder are MIT-licensed on GitHub.
